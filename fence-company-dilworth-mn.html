What changes the fence here

The work is residential lots, often narrower than a rural acreage, with open ground and wind on the east side of town. Privacy fences belong on the side and rear once the local height is confirmed.

Ground, rules, and the line

We do not publish a Dilworth height number we have not read off the city’s own sheet. What we will not do is apply Fargo’s 3-foot and 6.5-foot chart, or Moorhead’s 7-foot permit line, just because the towns share a highway. Before a tall fence is ordered, the Dilworth rule for that zoning gets checked.

Call 811 before digging. Minnesota locates run through Gopher State One Call. The soil is Red River Valley clay. Posts set at a depth that works in a southern city will heave here. Open lots east of the houses take more wind on a solid panel, so post spacing follows the panel.

If the lot is acreage rather than an in-town yard, say so. A rail or wire line is a different build from a vinyl privacy fence, and a pasture in Clay County is under Minnesota fence law.

Questions about fencing in Dilworth

Are Dilworth fence rules the same as Moorhead or Fargo?

No. Dilworth is its own city. Fargo’s land-development code stops at the state line, and Moorhead’s permit threshold is Moorhead’s. We confirm Dilworth’s current rule for the parcel before we order height. Bring any association sheet if the addition has one.

Will a wood privacy fence hold up east of town?

It will if the posts are deep and the wood gets a finish. Wind and sun are harder on an open lot than on a sheltered backyard. If you do not want to stain it, vinyl privacy is the closer match. Either one is set for frost heave, not just for the look of the boards.

Do you cross the river for a repair?

Yes. Dilworth is inside the area we cover from Fargo. A leaning post or a gate that drags is the same winter problem as on the North Dakota side. The locate ticket is the Minnesota one.
