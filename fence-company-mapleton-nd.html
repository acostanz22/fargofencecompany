What changes the fence here

An in-town yard usually wants privacy on the side and rear and a shorter open fence toward the street. An acreage lot wants rail or wire, plus a yard fence around the house. They should not be quoted as the same job.

Ground, rules, and the line

Mapleton is outside Fargo and outside West Fargo, so neither city’s height chart automatically applies. We check Mapleton’s rule, and any association rule on a newer plat, before panels are ordered. A fence that was legal on a West Fargo corner is not a template.

The ground is lake-plain clay. Spring melt sits in low spots. Posts go deep enough that frost does not jack them, and we skip holes that are still full of water. Open lots take more wind on a solid privacy fence than a lot tucked between houses.

If the property is a mix, we can fence the house yard in vinyl or wood and the field edge in rail or wire. Say which animals, if any, have to stay in. A picket will not do that job.

Questions about fencing in Mapleton

Is Mapleton covered, or only Fargo?

Mapleton is covered. It sits on the west side of the metro, a short drive from West Fargo. Tell us the address so we know whether the lot is in town or on acreage.

Can one property have two kinds of fence?

Yes. A privacy fence around the house yard and a rail or wire line on the acreage is a normal split. They are measured and priced as two runs because the posts, the gates, and the material are different.

Will a new subdivision tell me what fence I can build?

If there is an association, often yes. The city sets the legal limit. The association can require a material or a color inside that limit. Bring both. We build to the stricter one.
