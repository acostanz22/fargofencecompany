Fence company in Cass County, ND

Cass County outside the city limits is farmsteads, section-line fences, and rural home sites. City fence charts do not govern those parcels.

What changes the fence here

The jobs are longer, the gates are wider, and the ground is more often wet in spring. A backyard panel is the wrong product for a quarter-mile of pasture.

Ground, rules, and the line

Fargo, West Fargo, and Horace each have their own fence rules, and those rules stop at their city limits. An unincorporated parcel still has zoning, floodplain, and road right-of-way to respect. We confirm which jurisdiction the pin is in before we order a height. Guessing from the nearest city is how fences get built in the wrong place.

On agricultural land, North Dakota’s partition-fence law can require adjoining owners to share a boundary fence used for grazing. That is a neighbor agreement as much as a construction job. Sort out who is building which half before we set corners.

Lake clay and a high water table are the installation problem. Frost heave, not termites or salt air, is what wrecks a shallow post. Corners get braces. Gates clear the equipment. Low ground that holds water in April may need the line moved a few feet onto firmer soil.

Questions about fencing in Cass County

Do you fence rural Cass County, or only inside Fargo?

Both. Rural lines and small-town yards are part of the work, out to about a 25-mile drive. A long pasture is scheduled differently from a city backyard. Send the location and the approximate length.

Whose fence rules apply outside city limits?

Not Fargo’s residential handout, unless the lot is in Fargo. Township and county zoning, floodplain, and the road right-of-way still apply. We look up the parcel instead of borrowing a city number.

What fails first on a rural fence here?

Corners that were not braced, gates that are too narrow, and posts that were set shallow in wet clay. Wire stretch and animal pressure show those up. A repair that ignores the post will be back after the next spring thaw.
