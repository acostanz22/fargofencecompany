Vinyl fence installation in Fargo, ND

Vinyl privacy and picket fences for people who want the line to stay straight without a stain schedule. The posts are set for clay that freezes, not for a mild climate.

When this fence is the right job

Vinyl fits a backyard screen or a front-yard picket when painting is the chore you want off the list. It is a poor swap onto old wood posts. A vinyl line gets its own posts, set in concrete deep enough that frost does not jack them.

How the work goes

  1. 1Pick style and color. Privacy, picket, or a mix. White is the common stock color. A matched accent color is ordered, not pulled off a truck the same day.
  2. 2Check the city’s height. A solid 6-foot panel belongs on the side or rear in Fargo, not across a front yard. Horace and West Fargo use different numbers.
  3. 3Set the vinyl posts. Posts go in concrete on a new layout. We do not sleeve a vinyl system onto rotted wood.
  4. 4Assemble rails and panels. Privacy boards or pickets lock into the rails. Gates are built as part of the same run.
  5. 5Look at the bottom gap. Spring melt and a low corner can open a gap under a panel. We set the line so pets and snow do not write the gap for us.

Cold is the local test. Vinyl that is brittle in deep cold can crack if it is hit, and a dark color moves more in summer sun. We talk through color and which face of the yard takes the wind before the order is placed. A solid privacy run also catches prairie wind, so post spacing follows the panel, not a guess.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on the lot. These are the pieces that move the number. We do not publish a per-foot menu.

Style

Full privacy, semi-privacy, and picket do not share a price.

Height

A 4-foot picket and a 6-foot privacy line are different products, and the city may not allow both in the same yard.

Color

Standard white is easier to replace later. A custom color has to be ordered to match.

Wind

A solid panel takes more load than an open picket. Post spacing and depth follow that load.

Gates and tear-out

Each opening, and removal of the old fence, is priced on its own.

Access

Vinyl panels are bulky. A fenced backyard with no side access takes more time to carry in.

Questions about vinyl fencing

Does vinyl crack in a North Dakota winter?

Quality vinyl is made for cold, but it gets less forgiving below zero, and a hard impact can crack a rail. That is different from wood, which rots and twists. We set posts so the panel is not taking a frost heave, because a heaved post will split a vinyl rail faster than the cold will.

Can a vinyl privacy fence be 6 feet in a Fargo backyard?

On the side and rear, Fargo’s residential rules allow a fence up to 6.5 feet, and up to 8.5 feet in a rear yard if it sits at least 3 feet off the line. A standard 6-foot privacy panel fits the common case. The front yard does not. A solid front-yard fence stops at 3 feet.

Is vinyl only a privacy fence?

No. Picket and semi-private styles stay open, which is what a Fargo or West Fargo front yard usually needs. Privacy panels are the backyard screen. Many yards use picket on the street and privacy once the fence passes the front of the house.

Can you put new vinyl panels on my old wood posts?

Usually no. The vinyl system is built for its own posts, and the wood posts are often what failed over the winter. Bolting new panels to heaved posts repeats the lean. A vinyl replacement is a new post layout.

What changes the price of a vinyl fence?

Length, height, style, color, and the number of gates. Digging in wet clay, pulling an old fence, and carrying panels through a house add labor. We quote the measured line. We do not publish a per-foot price.
