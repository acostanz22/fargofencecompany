Privacy fencing

Privacy fence installation in Fargo, ND

Privacy fences for side yards and backyards, in wood or vinyl. The height is set by the yard and the city, not by the tallest panel in a photo.

When this fence is the right job

A privacy fence is the right job when you want a screen for a patio, a pool equipment pad, or the neighbor’s windows. It is the wrong job across a Fargo front yard. Solid front-yard fences stop at 3 feet. The tall line starts along the side, at or behind the front of the house.

How the work goes

  1. 1Place the tall section. We mark where the front-yard limit ends so a 6-foot panel does not run into the street yard.
  2. 2Choose wood or vinyl. Wood can be stained. Vinyl skips the stain and needs its own posts. Both are solid screens.
  3. 3Set posts for wind and frost. A solid panel catches prairie wind. Posts go deep in the clay, spaced for that load.
  4. 4Build the panels. Boards or vinyl privacy sections go on after the posts are set. The bottom stays clear of standing meltwater.
  5. 5Hang the gate you will actually use. A 3-foot walk gate does not fit a mower. We size the opening to what has to pass through it.

Fargo allows up to 6.5 feet on the side and rear in residential districts, and up to 8.5 feet in a rear yard if the fence is at least 3 feet off the property line. West Fargo’s side and rear limit is 6 feet. Horace requires a permit and keeps residential side and rear fences at 6 feet. We use the city on the permit, not a single metro number.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on the lot. These are the pieces that move the number. We do not publish a per-foot menu.

Material

Wood and vinyl privacy fences price differently and age differently.

Height

Six feet is the common panel. Going taller changes both the product and, in Fargo, the setback.

Wind

Open lots and corner lots load a solid fence harder than a sheltered backyard.

Setback

A taller Fargo rear-yard fence has to sit off the line. That loses a little yard and has to be planned.

Gates

Wider gates cost more and need heavier posts.

The city’s permit split

Fargo’s own pages do not match on when a permit starts. We flag anything over 6.5 feet and you confirm it with Inspections.

Questions about privacy fencing

How tall can a privacy fence be in Fargo?

In residential districts, side and rear fences may be 6.5 feet. A rear-yard fence at least 3 feet off the property line may go to 8.5 feet. The front yard is 3 feet if it is solid. The city’s inspections FAQ and the residential fence handout disagree on the permit threshold, 6.5 feet versus 8.5 feet. Ask Inspections before you build over 6.5 feet.

Should a privacy fence be wood or vinyl?

Wood if you want a stainable board fence and you will maintain it. Vinyl if you want a solid screen without that upkeep. Vinyl will not go on old wood posts. Both need posts set for frost and for the wind a solid panel takes on the prairie.

Will a solid fence blow over?

A shallow post will, especially on an open lot. The panel is a sail in a west wind. We set posts deep and space them for the panel. A sheltered yard between two houses is a milder case than a corner with a field beside it.

Which side of the fence faces the neighbor?

In West Fargo, the city’s fence rules say posts and supports face the owner’s side. Fargo does not use that same sentence. Good side, finished side, and who maintains the face are also things neighbors argue about. We set the finished face where you ask, and we follow the city rule when there is one.

Do I need a permit for a 6-foot privacy fence?

Inside Fargo, a typical 6-foot side or rear fence is under both numbers the city has published, so a permit is usually not the issue. Height, easements, and the corner sight triangle still are. Horace does require a fence permit. West Fargo should be checked against its own fence sheet. Bring the city, not just the panel height.
