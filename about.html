About Fargo Fence Company

We install and repair fences for houses, farms, and commercial lots in Fargo and nearby towns. The quote comes from the lot, not from a catalog price.

(701) 394-8468 Get a free quote

What we do

The work is wood, vinyl, aluminum, ornamental metal, chain link, farm fencing, gates, and repair. A front-yard picket and a pasture line are different products. We ask what the fence has to do before we name a material.

This is a service-area business. There is no public storefront and no published street address. Estimates are scheduled at the property. Call (701) 394-8468 or use the form.

How an estimate works

Tell us the city, the fence type, and a rough length. We look at access, gates, the old fence if there is one, and the ground. Fargo clay holds water, and shallow posts heave. That digging is part of the price, which is why a per-foot number from another climate does not transfer.

City rules do not transfer either. Fargo, West Fargo, Horace, and Moorhead each write their own height and permit lines. If an association has a fence sheet, bring it. We build to the stricter rule.
