Fence company in Harwood, ND

Harwood is north of Fargo, a small residential core with farmland around it. Wind and open sightlines matter more here than they do on a sheltered city block.

What changes the fence here

Yard fences for the houses, and rail or wire where the lot turns into a field. A tall solid fence on an open north lot has to be posted for wind, or it becomes a repair.

Ground, rules, and the line

Harwood is not in the City of Fargo. We do not use Fargo’s front-yard chart unless the parcel is actually in Fargo. The local rule, the right-of-way, and any easement get checked first. A corner fence still has to leave a view of the road.

Posts go into the same clay, and winter is the same winter. Shallow posts heave. On a field edge we brace corners and size the gate to the truck or the equipment, not to a sidewalk gate.

Chain link is a common choice when the job is a dog or a work area and the look is secondary. Vinyl or wood privacy fits a house yard if someone will live with a solid panel in the wind. We will say which one the lot is asking for.

Questions about fencing in Harwood

Do you install fences north of Fargo in Harwood?

Yes. Harwood is inside the area we cover. House-yard fences and farm lines are both common. Tell us which one, because the material changes.

Is a privacy fence a bad idea on an open lot?

It is a bad idea on shallow posts. A solid panel catches wind that a picket or a chain-link fence lets through. We space and set the posts for that. If you want a boundary and not a screen, an open fence is the simpler build.

Can you repair a line that leaned over the winter?

Yes, when the failure is a post or a section and the rest is sound. If the posts have heaved down the run, that stretch should be reset, not re-nailed. We will say which one it is after we see it.
